Triage step of the `CTT - Hygiene - Notify Owner` playbook. It reads the current hygiene issue, finds the owner from the `[owner: ...]` marker that every CTT rule and the daily sweep write into the description, looks the owner up in the CTT registry (name, role, Slack ID), quotes the guideline from the `CTT - Guidelines Index` list, and decides whether to assign the issue and whether to DM the owner. DMs follow `notify.mode` (`off`, `admins_only`, `all`) and `notify.max_dms_per_owner_per_day` in `CTT - Config`.
