#### Scripts

##### New: CTTSlackPost

Posts a message to the CTT Slack channel through the CTT Slack Webhook integration. A literal `\n` becomes a line break, so it also works from the War Room.

##### New: CTTMarketplaceUpdates

Jev-reviewed Marketplace updates, imported from the TypeSafe Jev Marketplace Guard (JavaScript). New `summary` action: a plain-text run summary for the CTT Slack channel.

#### Playbooks

##### New: CTT - Marketplace Updates

Collects newer pack releases, has TypeSafe Jev review them against the enabled integrations, installs approved updates with their dependencies, opens review cases for held or failed ones, and posts a summary to the CTT Slack channel with CTTSlackPost.

##### New: CTT - Marketplace Release Analysis

Sub-playbook: reviews one batch of releases with TypeSafe Jev (`jev-evaluate`).

#### Lists

##### CTT - Jobs

Adds the `CTT - Marketplace Updates` job (Monday to Thursday, 05:00 Singapore time). Run `!CTTEnsureJobs` after upgrading; on a tenant that already has a job with that name, it points the job at the pack playbook.
