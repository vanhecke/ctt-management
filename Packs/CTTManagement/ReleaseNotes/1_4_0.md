#### Playbooks

##### CTT - Hygiene - Notify Owner

After the DM the playbook now waits for the owner: the question "Did you fix it?" is asked by Generated link, and the DM carries the answer links (Slack buttons Fixed! / Not a violation / Open issue with `notify.buttons`). Fixed! runs the fix check: a verified fix, or a guideline without a check, is resolved with a close note; otherwise the question is asked again and the next DM says what is still wrong. Not a violation resolves the issue as False Positive and tells the CTT channel.

##### New: CTT - Hygiene - Fix Check

Runs `CTTHygieneFixCheck` every hour (job of the same name).

##### New: CTT - Hygiene - Reminders

Runs `CTTHygieneFixCheck remind=true` every day at 10:00 SGT (job of the same name).

#### Scripts

##### New: CTTHygieneFixCheck

Checks open CTT hygiene issues from the correlation rules and resolves the fixed ones with a close note saying why: GEN-01 registry entries, TAG-01/TAG-04 renamed or deleted objects, TAG-02 policy scopes, OOTB-01 re-attached built-in playbooks and scripts, LAB-01 endpoint ranges. Also handles the owner's Fixed! and Not a violation answers and sends the daily digests.

##### New: CTTHygieneAskLinks

Returns the newest answer links of the Notify Owner question for the DM.

##### CTTHygieneSendDM

New arguments `fixed_url`, `not_violation_url`, `buttons` and `note`.

##### CTTHygieneTriage

New output `CTT.Triage.buttons` (`notify.buttons` in `CTT - Config`).

##### CTTTenantStatus

The HYGIENE section shows closes by way, median time to fix per guideline, top fixers and the oldest open issue.

#### Integrations

##### CTT Slack Webhook

New action `msg_person_health_issue`: a DM with the buttons Fixed! (`fixed_url`), Not a violation (`not_violation_url`) and Open issue (`issue_url`). Needs a matching branch in the Slack workflow.

#### Agentix Actions

##### New: CTTHygieneConfirmFixAction

The CTT Assistant records a fix the user reports and runs the fix check (with the user's approval).
